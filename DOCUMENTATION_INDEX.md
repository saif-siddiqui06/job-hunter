## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start simple** - Just run it first, customize later
2. **Test with 1 email** - Before sending to 100
3. **Monitor spam folder** - Gmail sometimes flags legitimate emails
4. **Use App Password** - Never use regular Gmail password!
5. **Add delay** - 2-3 seconds prevents spam flagging
6. **Track results** - Know who you sent to
7. **Follow up** - 1 week later for better response rate
8. **Personalize** - Different subject = better results
9. **Monitor limits** - Free Gmail: ~500/day
10. **Be professional** - Genuine interest shows
